TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

Export-Grade Formats for Wholesale Distribution

Every package is engineered with food-grade multi-layer barriers designed to withstand transit humidity, temperature fluctuations, and long sea freight journeys.

1 kg Aroma-Lock Pouches

Multi-layer metallized foil pouches with resealable zip closure. Protects pods against UV radiation, oxygen ingress, and moisture. Perfect for premium retail shelves and boutique distributors.

Master Case: 20 kg (20 × 1kg) Shelf Life: 18 Months

5 kg Heavy Vacuum Packs

Co-extruded vacuum barrier poly packs sealed under negative pressure. Immobilizes cardamom pods during transit to prevent mechanical friction breakage, preserving pod integrity.

Master Case: 20 kg (4 × 5kg) Shelf Life: 24 Months

10 kg Export Cartons

5-ply heavy-duty virgin Kraft corrugated boxes with interior LDPE poly-liner and desiccants. High compression resistance for palletized ocean freight and air cargo.

Master Box: 10 kg Net Pallet Count: 60 Cartons

25 kg Bulk Gunny & PP Bags

Food-grade virgin woven polypropylene sacks equipped with 100-micron LDPE vapor-barrier inner bag. Designed for high-volume spice extractors, institutional buyers, and bulk repackers.

Weight: 25 kg Net FCL Loading: ~10 to 12 MT (20ft)
