Bodinayakanur Processing & Grading Facility

Our facility combines traditional spice mastery with cutting-edge mechanical sieve sorting and bichromatic color sorting technology.

Mechanical Sieve Screens

Multi-deck precision vibratory screen separators calibrated from 5.5mm to 9.0mm diameter, guaranteeing accurate pod classification and eliminating undersized pods.

Accuracy: ±0.1 mm Daily Capacity: 5+ MT

Optical Color Sorters

High-resolution bichromatic optical vision sensors that inspect pods individually to detect pale spots, thrips damage, insect bored pods, and split husks.

Purity: 99.5%+ Inspection: 360° Optical

Climate-Controlled Storage

Dedicated warehouse bays in Melachonathapuram, Bodinayakanur, maintained under strict humidity and temperature monitoring to preserve fresh cured aroma and chlorophyll green color.

Moisture: < 12% Location: Bodinayakanur
